Anna Su

Associate Professor · Henry N.R. Jackman Faculty of Law, University of Toronto
Cross-appointment, Department of History (by courtesy)
Research Lead, Schwartz Reisman Institute for Technology and Society

My research spans the law and history of international human rights law, comparative constitutional law, technology and international law, and law and religion. I am currently working on the human rights implications of artificial intelligence, digital constitutionalism, and AI alignment.

I hold an SJD from Harvard Law School, where my dissertation was awarded the John Laylin Prize for best paper in international law, and JD and AB degrees from the Ateneo de Manila University. I have clerked for the Philippine Supreme Court and served as consultant to the Philippine government negotiating panel with the Moro Islamic Liberation Front.
